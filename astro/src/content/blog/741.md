<p><span style="font-weight: bold;">Preemptive war</span> (or a preemptive strike) is waged in an attempt to repel or defeat a perceived inevitable offensive or invasion, or to gain a strategic advantage in an impending (allegedly unavoidable) war before that threat materializes. Preemptive war is often confused with the term preventive war. <sup><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources since May 2007" style="white-space: nowrap;"></span></sup></p>
