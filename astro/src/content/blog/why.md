We don´t speak clearly because we believe we will find our selves in the vortex of words.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe its an offense to things to put them bluntly.<br />&nbsp;We don´t speak clearly because we believe using another language put those horrid feelings away from us.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe pain and glory last longer when you avoid speaking of them.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe nobody is ready to understand us clearly.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe because hidding our selves is a sport in our country.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe interpretations can make us look better.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe this is our chance to use those words we can´t use in our mundane everyday conversations.<br />We don´t speak clearly because we believe poetry already exists in the content and it only needs the form to be complete. <br />We don´t speak clearly not because we are afraid but because we try to be brave and fail.<br /><br /><br />
