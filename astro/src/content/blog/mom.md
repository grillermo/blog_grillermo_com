I shall place my wooden sword in your back  
my little hand of war  
mine little eyes of water  
crushing the flats of your dress  
nosing your stomach wet

You shall place your tulip hand on my back  
your steel hand of love  
right bright eyes of air  
flattening the curls of my head  
breathing my sorrows away
