Them apples go on and on and on going about their redness, showing off their shine to the swallows so the birds pick them up and take on vacation, them apples tricksy little folks. I knew their kind before i met them, i had considered their attitude towards life in my plans but oh magicians undoing it all, always ruling those areas we traveling birds cannot fully appreciate so their advantage is complete, leviatanic superior hierachy powers falling upon us mere rational winged thinkers with plastic hearts.
